## 🕹️ About

Police Chief Simulator is a first-person police management and strategy game developed and published by Perfect Circle Team S.A. Set in Winston Springs, it places you in charge of a struggling county police station threatened by criminal influence. Register reports, investigate cases, coordinate patrol units, manage personnel, and expand the station as you work to restore order across the county. The game combines hands-on police activities with station development, detective work, resource management, and open-world operations.

Police Chief Simulator is for players who enjoy first-person simulation, detective work, management strategy, and methodical career progression.

## ✨ Features

- ⚖️ **Strategic Leadership** — Balance resources, personnel, investigations, and enforcement priorities.
- 🔎 **Case Investigations** — Register crimes, examine evidence, expand cases, and pursue suspects.
- 🗺️ **Winston Springs County** — Take control of districts and extend police influence across the county.
- 👮 **Personnel Management** — Hire officers, detectives, and office workers to expand your department.
- 🛠️ **Station Upgrades** — Improve facilities, equipment, and capabilities to handle tougher cases.
- 🚓 **Patrol Operations** — Deploy patrol units across Winston Springs districts to fight crime.
- 🏢 **Police Station Management** — Develop a small outpost into a fully operational county police station.
